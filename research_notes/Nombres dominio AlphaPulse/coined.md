### Inferences
- **Technalens** has the best overall balance: it explains the product category, sounds like software, avoids trading/execution implications, and has a clean exact-domain and exact-mark screen.
- **Pivotlume** is the most ownable and evocative option. It is less immediately descriptive than Technalens, but “pivot” gives it real technical-analysis relevance while “lume” provides visual identity.
- **Chartaris** is the strongest master-brand-style coinage. It could accommodate future universes and signal types without becoming semantically inaccurate.
- **Scanalens** is likely the easiest name to understand in a cold introduction, but its descriptive components may make it harder to protect than the more invented names.
- **Vectalens** should not advance unless an informal hear-and-type test shows that users consistently omit `or` and do not insert `r`.

### Gaps
- No live audience hear-and-type test was run. A five-person test should ask each participant to type the domain after hearing the name once, without seeing it.
- Pronunciation and meaning were assessed in English. `Chartaris`, `Pivotlume`, and `Trendalux` should also be checked with Spanish-speaking users before selection.
